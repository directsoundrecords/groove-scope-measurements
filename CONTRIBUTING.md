# Contributing

Submit a saved measurement through the globe menu in Groove Scope. Include the equipment identity, test record, description, photograph and the app-generated recap and eight fingerprint views. Reference track identifiers are optional; add them when known.

Submissions remain private until Direct Sound Records reviews them. Approved submissions are prepared as a GitHub update and appear after merge and website deployment. Acceptance is an editorial decision.

Preserve quality notes and record available tracking force, loading, alignment and repeatability context. Distinguish listening impressions from measured results.

Submit only material you have permission to publish under CC BY 4.0. Public attribution is optional; an omitted name is displayed as Anonymous contributor. No raw test-record audio is accepted.

For corrections or withdrawal requests, contact Direct Sound Records with the measurement ID. Historical numerical results are not silently rewritten.
