# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in Groove Scope Measurements.

External measurement submissions are not yet open. During the initial phase, all published measurements are created or directly supervised by Michelangelo Canonico for Direct Sound Records. This controlled phase is being used to stabilise the metadata schema, repeatability rules, app-version tracking, and acceptance criteria.

Corrections to published text, links, metadata, or transcription errors may be reported through a GitHub issue.

When community submissions open, contributors will be required to provide complete setup metadata, an original Groove Scope report, the Groove Scope and analysis-method versions, repeat captures where required, a declaration of test-record rights, and permission to publish the contribution under the repository licence.
