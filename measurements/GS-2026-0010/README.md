# GS-2026-0010 — Shure V15 V — 1 kHz Reference Check

**Measured by Michelangelo Canonico for Direct Sound Records using Groove Scope.**

An original Shure V15 V with a new JICO Custom Shop MR stylus gives an encouraging result in this Micro Seiki DQX500 / MA707 / Audio Research SP20 / Audient EVO4 capture: 33.33 RPM (−0.02%), 0.025% DIN-shaped wow and flutter, −0.01 dB channel balance and 27.3 dB average separation. THD measured 0.65% left and 0.49% right. The measurer describes clear, revealing sound before break-in and a surprisingly quiet background; the report calls for a repeat of the right-channel third-harmonic check before setup changes.

## Alignment and listening notes

This session pairs an original Shure V15 V cartridge with a new MR stylus from the JICO Custom Shop. The Micro Seiki DQX500 and MA707 feed an Audio Research SP20, with capture through an Audient EVO4.

The measurer reports an initial azimuth-related reading of roughly −3.5 dB. Using Groove Scope for iPhone, he quickly brought the indication within the app's tolerance, while feeling there was still room for finer adjustment. The exact dB quantity and an initial numerical record were not supplied. This observation is distinct from the final report's −0.01 dB signal channel balance and 1.70 dB crosstalk balance; no physical azimuth angle is recorded.

During adjustment, the measurer observed a decrease in harmonic distortion, particularly the third harmonic. No numerical before/after series was supplied. The final report notes that the right channel's third harmonic changed most during recording and advises a repeat before changing cartridge setup. A repeat capture would establish a firmer baseline before further refinement.

Michelangelo Canonico describes the sound as clear and revealing even before break-in, with a surprisingly quiet background. For him, this is an encouraging reminder of what a carefully aligned replacement stylus can bring to a classic cartridge. A comparison with modern stylus profiles is planned; this session supplies an initial baseline rather than a comparative ranking. The listening account is the measurer's assessment, separate from the report's numerical results.
