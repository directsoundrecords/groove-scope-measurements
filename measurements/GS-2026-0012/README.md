# GS-2026-0012 — Ortofon 2M Bronze / New Horizon — 1 kHz Reference Check

**Measured by Michelangelo Canonico for Direct Sound Records using Groove Scope Studio.**

The New Horizon averaged 33.41 RPM with the Ortofon 2M Bronze fitted. Groove Scope Studio’s Rotation Fingerprints show a recurring speed variation whose shape and angular position change over eight revolutions. The cartridge was measured as fitted, with no azimuth adjustment or setup tuning during the session.

## Azimuth, treble and trackability

The measurer reports that the cartridge body appeared tilted, the two channels differed in the upper frequencies and the setup failed the 70 µm tracking test. The supplied 1 kHz report documents channel balance, crosstalk and distortion. The frequency sweep and the tracking-test trace were not supplied, so the size of the treble difference and the conditions of the tracking failure remain unspecified.

Ortofon lists 80 µm tracking ability at 315 Hz for the 2M Bronze and identifies tracking force and anti-skate as factors in its lateral tracking test. The reported 70 µm failure warrants a closer look at this sample and setup. A cartridge or stylus issue remains possible. [Ortofon 2M Bronze specifications](https://ortofon.com/products/2m-bronze) · [Ortofon Test Record](https://ortofon.com/products/ortofon-test-record)

## Rotation Fingerprints

Both speed fingerprints show a slower portion and a faster portion that recur through eight turns, with changes in the position and shape of the pattern from turn to turn. That pattern describes pitch variation in this capture. A trend in mean platter speed, the contribution of the test record, and audibility in music were not established. The ±0.36% colour scale describes the plots. The report’s DIN-shaped wow and flutter figure is 0.053% RMS.

The eight original screenshots cover speed deviation, 1 kHz level modulation, opposite-channel leakage and harmonic distortion. Channel assignments follow the supplied filenames, and the angular reference between the left and right captures is unspecified.
