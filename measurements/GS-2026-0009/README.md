# GS-2026-0009 — BENZ SPJ — VYGER Atlantis / VYGER INERTIAL / ART USB Phono Plus — Hi Fidelity Milan

**Measured by Michelangelo Canonico for Direct Sound Records using Groove Scope.**

Measured as found in the CHARIO Academy room at Hi Fidelity Milan, with no setup adjustments: the BENZ SPJ / VYGER Atlantis / VYGER INERTIAL / ART USB Phono Plus chain averaged 33.33 RPM (−0.01%), with 0.032% DIN-shaped wow and flutter, 27.4 dB average separation and +0.49 dB channel balance. Harmonics were steady during recording. The measurer reports a successful 100 µm tracking-ladder pass and credits the new INERTIAL tonearm as a major contributor to that performance.

## Show session

Measured 4 October 2026 at 13:40 local time in the CHARIO Academy room at Hi Fidelity Milan. No setup adjustments were made.

Without any setup adjustments for this quick show-room measurement, the VYGER Atlantis / BENZ SPJ setup passed the 100 µm step on the test ladder, as reported by Michelangelo Canonico. It was an impressive demonstration of the tracking capability of the complete turntable, tonearm and cartridge combination in the room. This observation is additional session evidence; the supplied PDF documents the separate 1 kHz reference check.

## The new VYGER INERTIAL tonearm

The newly identified VYGER INERTIAL tonearm was central to the tracking demonstration. Michelangelo Canonico attributes much of the successful 100 µm tracking-ladder performance to this new arm, with no setup adjustments made for the session.

The supplied INERTIAL manual describes a movable counterweight supported by an air bearing and controlled laterally by magnetic forces. The manufacturer explains that this arrangement allows freer lateral movement when following record eccentricity. It also describes a shorter counterweight-to-pivot distance intended to reduce vertical inertia. These design features provide context for the strong tracking demonstration; this single session did not isolate the arm’s contribution from that of the cartridge and turntable.

Source: INERTIAL tonearm manual supplied by the measurer on 7 October 2026.

## Interpretation

Near-nominal average speed, closely matched output levels and steady harmonics provide an encouraging baseline from a quick event capture. Directional crosstalk differed by 3.03 dB; a longer documented follow-up could explore that difference. Tracking describes the complete turntable, tonearm and cartridge combination, not the turntable alone.
