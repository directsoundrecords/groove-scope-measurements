What the numbers reveal

Small movements shape a larger system

Speed consistency and low-frequency motion make hidden behaviour visible. Repeated observations can show how placement, operating state and setup changes alter that behaviour over time.

Scientific boundary

Measure motion without inventing its cause

Phone-measured vibration describes motion at the phone’s placement point. It is not a direct stylus or cantilever measurement. A calculated cartridge/tonearm resonance region adds context, but proximity between frequencies does not prove causation.

Method

Two views, kept distinct

RPM sessions describe rotational speed and unweighted wow and flutter. Vibration sessions describe 1–40 Hz motion under recorded conditions. Keeping them independent makes comparison clearer and preserves the meaning of each observation.
