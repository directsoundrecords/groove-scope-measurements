Scientific boundary

Phone-measured vibration describes mechanical motion at the phone placement point. It is not a direct measurement of stylus/cantilever or cartridge/tonearm resonance. Resonance Lab may compare measured vibration frequency content with a calculated resonance region, but that comparison is contextual evidence rather than proof of cause.
