Public archives

Two complementary views of analogue playback

Groove Scope examines the reproduced audio signal. Resonance Lab documents the mechanical system and its environment. Together they create a broader, traceable picture of vinyl playback behaviour.

Resonance Lab Measurements

Setup-centric observations of turntable mechanics, speed stability and low-frequency vibration, preserved as independent measurements over time.

  • Unweighted wow and flutter
  • 1–40 Hz mechanical vibration

How to read the archive

Measurements are evidence, not universal product ratings

Results belong to a documented physical setup under documented conditions. Component tolerances, setup choices, support, environment, test material, capture hardware and analysis-method versions can all affect the result.

Machine-readable JSON, CSV data, reports and version history are preserved in the underlying GitHub repositories. Unknown information is marked explicitly rather than inferred.
