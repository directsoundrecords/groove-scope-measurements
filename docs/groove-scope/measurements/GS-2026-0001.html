What we measured

One earlier capture, kept distinct

GS-2026-0001 documents a 1 kHz reference check made with a Rega P3, Rega RB330 tonearm, Audio-Technica AT-OC9XML moving-coil cartridge, Rega Neo PSU MK2 and the integrated phono stage of an Audio Research SP20. The capture entered Groove Scope through an interface labelled EVO4. The analysis covered settled test-tone frequency, calculated playback RPM, settled RPM range, DIN-weighted wow and flutter, unweighted speed variation, raw wow and flutter, channel balance, directional 1 kHz crosstalk, total harmonic distortion, second-to-fifth harmonic levels and relative residual noise. This is a historical record of one session on 12 July 2026. It does not describe the newer GS-2026-0003 capture, even though the principal turntable, arm and cartridge identities overlap. The test record, capture format, interface gain, actual SP20 phono loading, application version and analysis-method version were not recorded. Those gaps constrain comparison and remain visible in the technical archive. No rotation-fingerprint assets are published for this historical record.

What stands out

A speed result with diagnostics to repeat

The settled tone was 992.88 Hz and the calculated speed was 33.10 RPM, reported as -0.71% relative to nominal. The settled range extended from 32.63 to 33.55 RPM, while DIN-weighted wow and flutter was 0.045%. Those playback results were classified as stable primary observations in the structured record. Channel balance was -0.63 dB and average 1 kHz separation was 30.4 dB, with directional crosstalk of -27.7 dB left-to-right and -33.1 dB right-to-left. Combined THD was 0.31%; the archived channel values were 0.37% left and 0.34% right. The report’s interpretation separates the repeatable playback-speed evidence from channel-specific and directional diagnostics. It recommends confirming the speed result with another valid capture before correction, and repeating distortion, harmonic, balance and directional measurements before changing cartridge alignment, tracking force, anti-skate, azimuth or VTA. This page therefore preserves the evidence without converting it into a product judgement. The archive keeps that distinction visible for later valid comparisons.

Measurement limitation

Repeat channel-specific diagnostics before adjustment

The source record classifies channel balance, directional crosstalk, distortion and harmonic values as diagnostic or supporting evidence. Confirm them in another valid capture before using them to justify mechanical setup changes.
