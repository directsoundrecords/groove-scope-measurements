Behind the measurement

A vintage Technics under the stylus

The Technics SL-1410 MK2 and Empire 7000/III are documented here with ART USB Phono Plus, identified in the report title. Groove Scope follows a 1 kHz tone to measure mean speed, rotational variation, channel behaviour and harmonic structure.

The source report preserves the complete numerical capture. The rotation-fingerprint panels are reserved for the correct screenshots, which will add an angular view of the measured behaviour.

What stands out

Near-nominal speed and steady harmonics

The Technics SL-1410 MK2 / Empire 7000/III capture measured 33.33 RPM (−0.01%) with 0.040% DIN-shaped wow and flutter RMS through ART USB Phono Plus. Average separation was 23.8 dB at 1 kHz, with −0.26 dB channel balance. Harmonic levels were steady during recording; THD measured 2.26% left and 1.85% right.

Directional crosstalk measured −24.9 dB L→R and −22.8 dB R→L. The report describes harmonic levels as steady during recording and suitable for setup guidance. The higher left-channel THD is a useful focus for the next capture.

Review summary

Mean speed close to nominal, with clear channel differences

The Technics SL-1410 MK2 / Empire 7000/III capture measured 33.33 RPM (−0.01%) with 0.040% DIN-shaped wow and flutter RMS through ART USB Phono Plus.
