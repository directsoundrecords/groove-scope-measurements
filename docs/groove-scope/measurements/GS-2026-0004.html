Behind the measurement

A vintage playback chain, viewed one rotation at a time

This Micro Seiki DQX-500 and Goldring 2100 session follows a 1 kHz reference tone through playback speed, level, leakage and harmonic distortion. The supplied fingerprints show eight consecutive rotations, with right-channel alignment reported as +140°.

The speed profiles show a recurring broad rise and fall around the rotation. The median profile makes this pattern easier to compare across captures; identifying its cause requires further evidence.

What stands out

Near-nominal speed and steady harmonic levels

The Micro Seiki DQX-500 / Goldring 2100 capture measured 33.36 RPM (+0.09%) with 0.032% DIN-shaped wow and flutter RMS. Average separation was 21.8 dB at 1 kHz and channel balance was +0.32 dB. Harmonic levels were steady during the recording, with THD of 1.37% left and 1.97% right.

Directional crosstalk measured −22.4 dB L→R and −21.3 dB R→L. The report describes the harmonics as steady during recording and suitable for setup guidance. A repeat session can establish whether these channel differences persist.
