Without any setup adjustments for this quick show-room measurement, the VYGER Atlantis / BENZ SPJ setup passed the 100 µm step on the test ladder, as reported by Michelangelo Canonico. It was an impressive demonstration of the tracking capability of the complete turntable, tonearm and cartridge combination in the room. Behind the measurement

A quick check in the CHARIO Academy room

This capture was made at Hi Fidelity Milan on 4 October 2026 at 13:40, using an ART USB Phono Plus with the BENZ SPJ cartridge and VYGER Atlantis turntable. No adjustments were made to the setup for the measurement: this is a record of the system as it was presented in the room.

The successful 100 µm tracking-ladder step was the session’s highlight. Alongside the reference capture, it made for a memorable demonstration of what this complete analogue setup could do under show conditions.

What stands out

Near-nominal speed and steady harmonics

Average speed was 33.33 RPM (−0.01%), with 0.032% DIN-shaped wow and flutter RMS. Channel balance was +0.49 dB and average 1 kHz separation reached 27.4 dB. THD measured 0.96% left and 1.06% right; Groove Scope classified the harmonics as steady during recording and suitable for setup guidance.

Directional crosstalk was −25.9 dB L→R and −28.9 dB R→L, a reported 3.03 dB difference. This gives a useful baseline for a longer follow-up session, while preserving the central fact that the show setup was left untouched.

Review summary

An impressive demonstration, measured as found

Measured as found in the CHARIO Academy room at Hi Fidelity Milan, with no setup adjustments: the VYGER Atlantis / BENZ SPJ chain averaged 33.33 RPM (−0.01%), with 0.032% DIN-shaped wow and flutter, 27.4 dB average separation and +0.49 dB channel balance. Harmonics were steady during recording. The measurer also reports a successful 100 µm tracking-ladder pass.

The unchanged setup and successful tracking demonstration make this a valuable show-room snapshot. A longer repeat with the test-record and setup details documented would build on this encouraging result.
