Behind the measurement

Two grams at the stylus. Eight revolutions under scrutiny.

The Rega P3 was positioned on a wall shelf for this session, with the AT-OC9XML’s overhang checked and confirmed correctly aligned on the RB330 arm. Tracking force was measured at 2 g. The signal then passed through the Audio Research SP20 preamplifier—the centrepiece of this playback chain—into the input identified in the report as EVO4.

With those setup details established, the interesting question is what happens as the groove comes around again. Groove Scope follows the 1 kHz tone across eight consecutive revolutions, placing speed, level, leakage and distortion against relative platter angle. A recurring feature invites a different follow-up from one that moves between rotations; the robust median profile helps reveal what persists.

Here, near-nominal speed and strong channel separation sit alongside a changing left-channel third harmonic. Will that pattern return in a second capture with the same alignment and 2 g tracking force? That is the next comparison this carefully documented setup makes possible.

What stands out

Excellent cartridge separation in a near-nominal-speed chain

The AT-OC9XML is the standout in the channel results. This capture measured 31.8 dB average separation at 1 kHz, with 30.2 dB left-to-right and 33.5 dB right-to-left; Audio-Technica publishes 27 dB at 1 kHz. The measured channel-balance magnitude is 0.24 dB, comfortably inside the manufacturer’s 1.0 dB figure. The Rega P3 measured 33.34 RPM, +0.02% relative to 33 1/3 RPM, with a settled range of 33.26–33.42 RPM and 0.030% DIN-shaped wow and flutter. THD is 0.29% on the left and 0.37% on the right. The left channel’s moving third-harmonic pattern gives a repeat capture one focused question to revisit without diminishing the excellent separation and balance demonstrated here.

Review summary

Excellent cartridge separation anchors the result

The AT-OC9XML delivered 31.8 dB average separation at 1 kHz—4.8 dB above Audio-Technica’s published 27 dB figure—while the measured 0.24 dB channel-balance magnitude sits comfortably inside the manufacturer’s 1.0 dB specification. The Rega P3 added 33.34 RPM (+0.02%) and 0.030% DIN-shaped wow and flutter. THD measured 0.29% left and 0.37% right; the moving left-channel H3 pattern is the one area worth sharpening with a repeat capture.
