What we measured

A 1 kHz reference check with an incomplete setup record

GS-2026-0002 records a 1 kHz test-signal capture from a Thorens TD160 Super fitted with an SME III tonearm and Ortofon OM10 cartridge. The documented playback chain includes a Perreaux EP2 phono preamplifier and an input labelled EVO4. Groove Scope analysed playback RPM, settled speed range, the error relative to nominal speed, DIN-shaped wow and flutter, unweighted speed variation, raw wow and flutter components, channel balance and directional electrical crosstalk. Distortion and second-to-fifth harmonic measurements are also retained in the structured archive because they are part of the source report, but their quality classification materially limits interpretation. The test-record edition and track, tracking force, anti-skate, cartridge loading, interface settings, capture format, application version and analysis-method version were not recorded. This page therefore describes one draft measurement session from 22 July 2026. It cannot establish a general performance specification for the Thorens, SME or Ortofon components, and it does not fill missing setup context from another record.

What stands out

A clear speed observation and an unusable THD comparison

The capture reports 34.14 RPM, +2.41% fast relative to nominal, with a settled range of 34.06–34.23 RPM and a span of 0.17 RPM. DIN-shaped wow and flutter is 0.037%. The separate unweighted diagnostics report ±0.130% RMS raw speed variation and ±0.256% at two sigma, with raw wow and flutter RMS of 0.121% and 0.045%. Channel balance is +0.82 dB. Directional crosstalk is -24.9 dB left-to-right and -28.7 dB right-to-left, for 26.8 dB average separation. The distortion figures are not comparable evidence here: the report labels distortion quality variable and states that phase tracking did not retain enough clean, continuous tone segments for the experimental comparison. The archived left and right THD values must therefore not drive setup decisions. The structured interpretation recommends repeating the capture, checking the record for clicks or dropouts and confirming the fast-speed result in another valid measurement before correcting the deck. That sequence preserves the observation while respecting its stated reliability limits.

Measurement limitation

Distortion quality is variable

Phase tracking did not retain enough clean, continuous tone segments for the experimental THD comparison. The archived distortion and harmonic figures preserve provenance, but they are not suitable for setup decisions from this capture. Repeat the measurement and inspect the record for clicks or dropouts.
