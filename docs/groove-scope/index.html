What the numbers reveal

Performance is a relationship

Cartridge, alignment, tonearm, turntable, test record, phono stage and capture path all contribute to the result. That context is what makes a measurement useful for diagnosis, comparison and informed setup—not just another score.

Method

From test groove to result

Groove Scope analyses known test signals captured from the playback chain. Records retain the setup, conditions and analysis version needed to understand the result. Speed and wow-and-flutter, channel behaviour, crosstalk and harmonic measurements remain separate evidence types.

Archive status

Results can grow without losing their history

A record may be initial, repeated, comparative or revised. Later work adds context; it does not silently replace the original observation.
