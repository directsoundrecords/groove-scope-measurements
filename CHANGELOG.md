# Changelog

All notable repository and measurement-record changes are documented here.

## 1.1.9 — 2026-11-06

- Published the ImageGen retouch for the Rega P1 measurement and advanced GS-2026-0013 to 0.9.1.
- Restored the earlier photographic hero layout on the Rega P1 and New Horizon measurement pages.

## 1.1.8 — 2026-11-06

- Added GS-2026-0013, a 1 kHz reference check of the Rega P1 and Rega Carbon at the AudioReview laboratory.
- Documented the Groove Scope-assisted azimuth adjustment and the 60 µm and 80 µm tracking results observed with Groove Scope Studio at 2.0 g and 2.5 g, respectively. Archived the source report and eight rotation plots.

## 1.1.7 — 2026-11-05

- Added the setup photograph to GS-2026-0012 and advanced the record to 0.9.1. The archive uses a metadata-stripped JPEG and the public page uses resized WebP copies.

## 1.1.6 — 2026-11-05

- Added GS-2026-0012, a 1 kHz reference check of an Ortofon 2M Bronze on a New Horizon turntable with an ART preamp.
- Archived the source report and eight rotation fingerprints. The record documents the setup as measured, the reported 70 µm tracking result and the changing speed pattern across consecutive rotations.

## 1.1.5 — 2026-11-05

- Added GS-2026-0011, a 1 kHz reference check of an Audio-Technica VM95SH on a Thorens TD 160 Super with an SME III tonearm.
- Documented the Groove Scope-assisted azimuth adjustment, the bent cantilever that limits the cartridge findings, and the speed results that warrant follow-up on the Thorens.
- Archived the source report and eight fingerprint plots, and added the setup photograph with subtle tonal adjustments to the web presentation.

## 1.1.4 — 2026-10-18

- Added GS-2026-0010: original Shure V15 V with a new JICO Custom Shop MR stylus, measured on a Micro Seiki DQX500 / MA707 through an Audio Research SP20 and Audient EVO4.
- Preserved the original PDF, HEIC and eight channel-labelled fingerprint PNGs, with lossless web plots, a cosmetically retouched hero and its edit prompt.
- Published the full measured results, attributed alignment and listening notes, and the report's right-channel H3 repeat advice; added the entry to both catalogues and discovery indexes.

## 1.1.3 — 2026-09-05
