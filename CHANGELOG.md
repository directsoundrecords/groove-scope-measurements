# Changelog

All notable repository and measurement-record changes are documented here.

## 1.1.18 — 2026-11-09

- Published GS-2026-0017, a 1 kHz reference check of an Ortofon 2M Bronze on a vintage Thorens TD 160 Super with an SME III tonearm and ART preamp.
- Archived the original report, eight rotation fingerprints and setup photograph. The final capture averaged 32.2 dB separation, and the record documents the reported azimuth and anti-skate adjustments.

## 1.1.17 — 2026-11-09

- Redesigned the public library header with a shorter title, a concise introduction, and a compact app download area.

## 1.1.16 — 2026-11-09

- Moved the public library’s catalogue summary into the header and added best-first sorting for playback speed, speed error, DIN-shaped wow and flutter, and channel separation.

## 1.1.15 — 2026-11-09

- Corrected the tonearm model for GS-2026-0014 to SME 309 across the public archive and Studio Reference Library.

## 1.1.14 — 2026-11-09

- Standardised titles across all 13 Groove Scope records, the public archive and the Studio Reference Library. Titles lead with the documented cartridge, and the turntable and tonearm appear in the playback setup.

## 1.1.13 — 2026-11-09

- Published GS-2026-0016, a 1 kHz reference check of the AVID Reference Ruby, Acutus and Nexus.
- Archived the source report showing 37.6 dB average separation, eight rotation plots and the Groove Scope Studio tracking report. The measurer observed clean tracking at 90 and 100 µm and an isolated spike at 80 µm.

## 1.1.12 — 2026-11-09

- Published GS-2026-0015, a 1 kHz reference check of the Takumi TT Level 2.1 DC and Takumi Aka MC.
- Archived the source report and eight rotation plots. The record documents accurate average rotation at 33.33 RPM, crosstalk after azimuth adjustment, and session observations of excellent vibration rejection and moderate trackability.

## 1.1.11 — 2026-11-07

- Published GS-2026-0014, a 1 kHz reference check of the SME Model 12 MkII with a Charisma Audio MC-1 Alpha cartridge at Entertainment Machine in Rome.
- Archived the source report and eight rotation plots. The report measured 33.32 RPM (−0.04%) and found a 5.57 dB difference between crosstalk directions.

## 1.1.10 — 2026-11-06

- Separated editorial release status from the availability of setup metadata.
